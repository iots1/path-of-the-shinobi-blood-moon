> Moved verbatim out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-22 (it was over the size limit). Beat 1 raise, the two-handed off-hand wrist range pass. Older attack design notes (beats 1–3, K, K K, L, blink) are in `architecture-details.md`; the reach rescale of every key is in `model-2026-10-18.md`.

- **Beat 1 (一) raise re-shaped (2026-10-20) — right shoulder/elbow/hand.** Measured before: at the wind-up the right upper arm never got above −6° (elbow below the shoulder, tucked to the centre line), G only at chest-local y .42 (face height), and key 0's level blade (el 15) read as a forward jab at 100–130 ms. Relaxing `liftCost` (tried `liftFree` 15 live) changed nothing — the low elbow came from the key's hand/blade target, not the lift cost. Changes: key 0 el 15→50 (hy .06, hz .24) so the tip rises continuously; wind-up key (190) → `hx -.04, hy .54, hz .14, by 0, el 135` (G above the forehead, blade back over the head; picked from a static-hold scan), upper arm now peaks ~+28°; new `SHOULDER.raise` (.24 rad) lifts the RIGHT clavicle in `driveRealBones` by `S.shRaiseR` (0 below −15° upper-arm elevation, 1 at +35°, eased 60 ms, computed after the right arm solve and applied next frame). **Side effect found and fixed:** either change alone made beat 2's off-hand let go for ~75 ms (530–610 ms) in EVERY combo (crossL trip: left wrist ~1 cm past −.14 at beat 2's start) — the old code only did that on the first combo after a reload. Fixed by moving beat 1's follow-through key `hx -.01 → .02`. Result: `m.run()` 55 PASS / 0 FAIL on several fresh reloads (combo two-hand grip min 0.81–0.86 — the two documented grip FAILs no longer trip, but the margin over the 0.8 limit is thin). **Not done:** from the front, the LEFT forearm still crosses in front of the face at the top of the wind-up (left elbow held low by `liftCost` in the off-hand solve); a matching left-clavicle raise / left elbow lift is the next step if wanted.

- **Two-handed off-hand wrist brought into range (2026-10-21, 5th pass) — supersedes the "KNOWN … ~3×" note above.** Diagnosis (per-frame, `armAngles('L')` while `twoEff>.5`): with the grip seat fixed, the left wrist is only in range for a left-forearm↔blade angle of ~40–85°; the cuts swung it over 15–147°, and even the best roll around the hilt left 49/78 combo frames out of range — so roll (`twoRoll`, `rollByWrist`) is NOT the lever; pose and seat are. Fixes: (1) **seat flex** — the off-hand may turn about its palm normal through the palm's contact point (`GRIP_TUNE.seatFlex` 30°, `S.dL`, chosen each frame by `wristEllipse`, eased 60 ms, 0 off the hilt; applied to the two-handed `gripPass` (`gL2d`), the elbow cost (`qHL`) and `aimHand`); (2) **keys**: beat 2 (二) last key by −30/el 100 → −10/70; beat 3 (三) strike/follow-through by 66/78 → 30/35 with the torso unwound further (chestYaw .44/.46 → .65/.67, hipYaw .30/.32 → .42/.44; world blade still ends 67–73° left of facing), strike hands hx .10 → 0, el −3 → −10; L (一閃) strike/hold by 70/72 → 20/22, chestYaw → .75/.77, hipYaw → .5/.52, strike hx 0 / el −10 (winding its wind-up further made the off-hand grab ~100 ms late — reverted). Result: off-hand wrist out of range 73 % → 7 % of held frames in the combo (peak 289 → 138 %), 86 % → 11 % in L (343 → ~143 %), K / K K 0 %; the rest is the fastest 40–100 ms of beat 1's impact and the beat-3 / L strikes.
