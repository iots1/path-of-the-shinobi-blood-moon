# Face re-sculpt, nose, nostrils, outline + ears (2026-10-22)

> Moved verbatim out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-22 (it was over the size limit). Headless Blender pipeline in `blender-ninja/face_sculpt_scripts/`. **If the hands/body are re-exported, re-run the face pipeline on the new glb.**

- **Face re-sculpt (2026-10-22) — `assets/ninja.glb` was 26,007 verts / 37,828 tris after this (was 23,232 / 33,434), 66 joints; the later palm-rig re-export (70 joints, 26,644 verts) kept the face — checked: face positions ≤0.0007 mm, textures byte-identical.** Reference `art/reference/ninja-no-hood/ninja_no_hood_front.jpeg`; the Meshy face was flat and round (no brow ridge/nasion, jowls, jaw flowing into the neck). Backup: `assets/raw/backup-before-face-sculpt-2026-10-21/` (glb + index.html + HEAD). Before/after: `out/face-sculpt-before-after.png`. Scripts: `blender-ninja/face_sculpt_scripts/` (headless: `Blender -b --factory-startup --python face_edit.py -- IN.glb OUTPREFIX --smooth 0 --clearcn --feat feat_v3.json --export mesh.glb`, then `python3 splice.py IN.glb mesh.glb full.glb`, then `python3 flatten_normal.py full.glb final.glb`).
  - **Geometry:** UV-seam duplicates welded on the head only → 2 levels of **Loop subdivision** on the face triangles (5 mm outer face, 2.5 mm eyes/nose/mouth/chin; the zone rim stays fixed) → position-only "brushes" (`feat_v3.json`: Gaussian blobs in front-projected x/z or 3D, along the normal or ±Y): brow ridge, under-brow socket, straight narrow nose dorsum + tip + alar groove, cheekbones + hollow below, slimmer jowls (V-line, max 5 mm), jaw angle kept, underside of the jaw lifted (crisp jawline), mouth slit/lip volume/philtrum, mentolabial fold, chin forward. UVs and skin weights are only interpolated, never edited — the painted texture stays put.
  - **Splice, not a Blender re-export:** `splice.py` keeps the ORIGINAL vertex buffer + triangles verbatim and only appends the triangles that don't exist in the original (+ their new verts); skin/animations/images/nodes are byte-identical (a no-op run reproduces the original mesh data byte-for-byte). Sidesteps the 24/30 fps clip re-export problem and can't touch the hands. **If the hands/body are re-exported later, re-run the face pipeline on that new glb** (it is not baked into any .blend).
  - **Normal map flattened under the face/neck skin** (`flatten_normal.py`, image `texture_0_normal` only, 113k px): the blocky light/dark patches on the face in-game came from Meshy's baked tangent-space normals per UV island (pre-existing — the original glb has them too; `normalScale` 0 live made the face clean). Hair/cloth keep their normal map; `texture_0` (albedo, incl. the hand patches) untouched.
  - **Dead ends (don't redo):** Phong-tessellated midpoints and a thin-plate (bi-Laplacian) fill both showed pits/folds — the Meshy surface has ~1 cm positional noise, and the bi-Laplacian slid new verts sideways at the zone rim and tore the painted eyebrows into "tents"; `normals_split_custom_set` after the edit gave pitted shading (clear the custom normals instead; `splice.py` restores the original normals outside the edit). **A Workbench `bpy.ops.render.render()` through the Blender MCP crashed the user's Blender** — render headless (`Blender -b`) instead; it works there.
  - Verified: in-game close-ups front/3-4/side (`I.stop()`, set `CAM.yaw/pitch/zoom/focus`, `I.frame(0,0)` — `CAM.focus` is re-lerped to the hips every live frame), `m.run()` 0 FAIL, `m.views` run/walk/combo checked.
  - **Nose pass (2026-10-22, user: the nose still read as unclear) — glb now 29,213 verts / 42,630 tris, 70 joints.** Built on the palm-rig glb (spliced, so its hands/bones are byte-identical). (1) Geometry: a 3rd Loop level on the nose only (`--levels 3`, ≈1.25 mm) + `feat_nose4.json`: tip lobule + supratip break, crisper dorsum/steeper side walls, alae (wings), shallow alar + supra-alar creases, nostrils (underside-gated), base narrowed 1.8 mm/side and raised 1.8 mm (the face overlay showed it wider/lower than the reference). **Deep creases let the inverted-hull outline poke through as black ticks** — keep grooves ≲0.5 mm and carry the line in the texture instead. (2) Texture (`paint_albedo.py` + `paint_nose6.json`, `texture_0` only, face triangles only): each texel is mapped back to its 3D point on the mesh, so paint follows the sculpt: `clean` blends Meshy's baked pink/grey lower-nose smudge (painted for the old shape) toward the bridge's median skin tone, `mul` darkens nostrils/alar crease/bridge side walls, and `mul` 1.18 lifts the down-facing nose base that the toon ramp otherwise turns into a brown block. Always paint from the UNPAINTED albedo (4th arg) so re-runs never stack. Pipeline: `face_edit.py IN out --levels 3 --clearcn --feat feat_nose4.json --export m.glb` → `splice.py` → `flatten_normal.py` → `paint_albedo.py OUT.glb FINAL.glb paint_nose6.json UNPAINTED.glb`.
  - **`/debug/face-overlay.html`** (user's requested check: in-game render under the reference with an opacity slider, iterate against it): the game renders the standing face with an ortho camera; front view is anchored on the pupil mid-point and scaled by the pupil distance, side view on the eye and scaled by eye→chin; refs = `assets/reference/face-{front,side}.jpeg` (copies of `art/reference/ninja-no-hood/ninja_no_hood_{front,side}.jpeg`); yellow = reference landmarks, pink = model landmarks (bind-space vertices picked by `MODEL`), offsets in mm in the status line; "แคป PNG" saves to `out/`. Front and side disagree on the nose height, so part of it is head pitch / the two references' framing, not shape — use the pitch slider before sculpting from the side numbers.
  - **Face v2 (2026-10-22) — new reference `art/reference/ninja-no-hood/ninja_face.jpeg` (front + ~75° side, high-res); glb 29,310 verts / 42,630 tris, 70 joints, built on the hand-proportion glb.** The overlay is now on this ref by default (`assets/reference/face-ref2.jpeg`; old refs under "ref เก่า"), model landmarks are picked by **texture coordinate** (`MODEL` = UVs; a bind-position pick lands on the wrong vertex once the mesh is re-sculpted), and `face-overlay.html?model=/assets/….glb` previews any glb (uses the game's `?motionOverlay=1&model=` hook) without touching `assets/ninja.glb`. (1) **Proportions (user chose "match the ref")**: at equal pupil distance the Meshy lower face was ~2 cm short (eye→chin 102 vs 122 mm). `face_edit.py --stretch stretch_v2.json`: monotone-cubic z remap (eye level fixed; nose base −6, mouth −12, chin −23 mm), full on the front of the face, faded out toward the ears (`yfade`) and onto the neck by Head weight (`headw`); plus `feat_jaw_undo2.json` (jaw −3.5 mm/side, and most of the nose-pass nostril/crease/alae/tip bumps undone — the photo now carries those, and the bumps made double shadows). Overlay after: nose base 1.2, mouth 0.8, chin 0.8 mm off. (2) **`project_photo.py proj_v3.json`**: projects the ref's front photo into `texture_0` on the face triangles — each texel's 3D point (x, z) goes through a thin-plate spline fitted on 19 landmark pairs (model mm, read off a flat 8 px/mm render → photo px), blended by facing-front × inside a feathered face outline in the photo × not-model-hair (hair test only above the brows / at the temples — the painted brows/eyes are dark too and must be replaced), photo colour mean-matched to the model's skin (contrast kept), unsharp-masked. The nose zone also takes the photo on down-facing surfaces but not on side-facing ones (a front photo smeared over the alar sides = grey-blue streak). Pipeline: base glb → `face_edit.py --levels 0 --clearcn --feat feat_jaw_undo2.json --stretch stretch_v2.json --export m.glb` → `splice.py` → `flatten_normal.py` → `project_photo.py`. **Re-run on any new base exactly once** (the stretch is not idempotent). Game shader note: `applyBodyShading`'s skin mask is colour-based (r−b ≥ .13, r ≥ .38); only ~5% of projected face texels fall outside it and they're the genuinely dark ones (nostrils, pupils, brow hairs). Remaining vs ref: side view — softer jaw→neck line (no crisp gonial angle), chin less projected; front — eyes read rounder, a toon shadow band on the nose tip, slight smear at the right mouth corner.
  - **Nostrils (2026-10-22, user: from a low camera the nose needs real nostrils) — glb 33,185 verts / 50,094 tris.** On top of face v2 (same base `st4_full` = hand-proportion glb + stretch, before flatten/projection). (1) `face_edit.py --levels 4`: a 4th Loop level on the nose base only (|x|<13 mm, z 1.550–1.583, ≈0.6 mm edges) + `feat_nostril5.json`: one teardrop cavity per side (3D blob with new `rxy` = rotation in the horizontal plane 30°, toward the tip, and `pow` = flat-bottomed profile), only 1 mm deep and gated to down-facing normals. **Deeper (3–4 mm) cavities make the inverted-hull outline poke out of the nostrils as black jagged ticks** in-game; the opening is carried by paint instead. Level 4 left needle triangles on its rim (white specks): `face_edit.py` now collapses triangles with min angle < 4° after refining. (2) `project_photo.py proj_v4.json`: new `nose_zone.down` keeps the front photo OFF down-facing nose surfaces (it smeared a grey-blue streak / the photo's baked under-nose shadow across them). (3) `paint_albedo.py paint_nostril6.json` (run on the projected glb, with itself as the unpainted source): `clean` the underside + subnasale to skin tone, dark nostril interiors with the new per-blob `down` gate (only texels whose normal points down — this nose's underside slopes forward, so the gate is loose, [.22,.5]; a strict gate lost the nostrils from below, none made them read as two dark holes from the front).   - **Outline + ears (2026-10-22; user reviewed a Gemini list — particle hair / separate eyeballs / Subdivision-above-Solidify don't apply to this pipeline: no particle export to glTF, a 2nd mesh/material breaks the loader, the outline is code not a modifier).** glb 33,133 verts / 50,082 tris. Diagnosed in-game by zeroing the outline: every black tick/flap on the head (nostrils, hairline, ear) IS the inverted-hull outline. (1) **`_OUTLINE_NORMAL`** (`add_outline_normals.py`): one normal per position, angle-weighted, copies welded only if they face the same side and inward-wound faces flipped to the corner's normal (Meshy double layers made a naive average collapse); `loadNinja()` renames GLTFLoader's `_outline_normal` → `outline_normal` (keeps volume_skinning.js's `normal * 0.0080` match) and extrudes along it, falling back to NORMAL if the glb lacks it. **Blender exports drop it — run `add_outline_normals.py` LAST on every new glb.** It barely changed the look (seam tears weren't the main cause). (2) **The actual fix: outline depth bias** `real.outlineBias` (uniform, 6 mm view-space, live-tunable `__iso.rig.real.outlineBias.value`): the hull is drawn a few mm further from the camera, so it shows only past the silhouette, not poking out of concave spots in front. 6 mm cleared the nostril ticks; 12 mm also thins some hair-edge lines but eats more silhouette — kept 6. Remaining thin dark lines along the hairline/side are real edges of the Meshy hair shell (go away only with new hair). (3) Ears: `face_edit.py --del-islands 40` (deleted the 12-face loose shard that was the black triangle on the forehead) + `--relax relax_ears3.json` (Taubin in spheres, `boundary:true` also moves open-edge verts; folds > 70° near the right ear 20 → 9; locate with `find_folds.py`); `fix_hair_specks.py`: hair-majority triangles around the ears get their skin-coloured texels (UVs straddling the ear's skin island = pink stripes/shards) repainted with the triangle's median hair colour, texels used by skin-majority triangles protected (1,390 px). Pipeline for this pass: current glb → `face_edit.py --levels 0 --clearcn --del-islands 40 --relax relax_ears3.json --export` → `splice.py` (images come from the input glb) → `add_outline_normals.py` → `fix_hair_specks.py`.
