Motion

Camera focus: Body

Controls

  • WASD or click ground to run, hold Shift to walk
  • J slash (press repeatedly for a 3-beat combo)
  • Space dash (short warp)
  • K lunging thrust (attack 2)
  • L finisher: 一閃 single cut (the target falls after the blade stills)
  • C arm "Capture 8": the next attack is shown as an 8-frame sheet; Download saves it + a skeleton JSON
  • QE rotate camera  ·  RF high/low angle
  • B skeleton  ·  T slow-mo  ·  N day / night
  • P cycle the loop mode (run/walk, one-way or back-and-forth, or an attack: repeats in place, for tuning poses live)
  • +- or Cmd/Ctrl + +- quick zoom in / out (tap = one step, hold = continuous)  ·  same as the zoom buttons on the right
  • Scroll wheel to zoom (down to a face / hand close-up), click-drag (or right-click drag) to free rotate
  • Alt+drag (or middle-drag) pans the camera up/down/sideways
  • V camera focus: body → head → sword hand → feet  ·  0 reset camera
